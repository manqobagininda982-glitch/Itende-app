Sound output

For AUX output, connect the phone's headphone output or a compatible USB-C audio adapter to your speakers. MIDI carries note instructions, not audio; the phone generates the tone and plays the beat together.

Low-latency Web Audio is enabled, but the phone, browser, audio adapter and speaker connection still affect the final delay. For the lowest delay, keep the studio open and use a wired audio output.
